Welcome to ONIFITILA FREE ICT, Where cutting-edge technology meets hands-on learning Located in Maryland, Lagos, Nigeria.

We are dedicated to empowering future leaders in information and communication Technology(ICT). Since [10 April, 2021], We have been at the fore-front of providing comprehensive education and training in the rapidly evolving ICT landscape.


OUR MISSION

At Onifitila ICT, our mission is to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and pratical experience neccessary to thrive in the digital age.

We aim to foster innovation, critical thinking, and ethical pratices in ICT through a dynamic curriculum and a supportive learning environment.

OUR VISION

At Onifitila ICT, our vision is to be a premier ICT institution, empowering individuals and organisations to succeed in the digital age. We envision a future where technnology is harnessed to drive innovation, growth, and sustainable development.

OUR GOAL

Our goal is to provide cutting-edge education, training, and research in ICT, producing graduates who are equiped to tackle complex challenges and create a positive impacts in our communities.
We strive to foster a culture of excellence, creativity, and social responsibility, preparing the next generation of ICT professionals to shape the future of technnology and society.
